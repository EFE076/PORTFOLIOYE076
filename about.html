Who am I?

My name is Efe Karatay and I am a Creative Software Development student. I like working with technology but I also enjoy being creative with the things I make.

I am interested in websites, games and interactive projects. I like learning new things by actually making something and testing if it works.

02

My Education

I study Creative Software Development at Grafisch Lyceum Rotterdam. During my study I learn how to create websites, work with code and design digital products.

I have worked with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, databases and different design programs. During projects I also learn how to turn an idea into something that actually works.

03

My Minor

My minor is Immersive Design. This minor is mostly focused on creating interactive experiences and game development.

I chose this minor because game development interests me. I like the combination of programming and design because you can actually see and interact with what you have created.

04

My Future

In the future I want to become better at software development and build bigger projects. I especially want to learn more about game development and interactive software.

With this education I want to get enough experience to work as a developer and eventually be able to create my own projects and ideas.
